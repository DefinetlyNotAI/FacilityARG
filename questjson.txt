Quest Line 7 -> Bonus Content [aka Sagas]
These remain hidden until the *Facility* is completed 100%.
Each saga consists of a linear quest chain, meaning quests must be completed in order. However, only the **first quest** of each saga determines the fate of the rest:
 If the first quest of a saga fails, all quests within that saga are automatically marked as failed.
 Other sagas remain unaffected — a failure in Saga 1 doesn't impact Saga 2 or others.
Whenever a saga begins, the previous one gets stopped and all pending quests of the previous saga gets FAILED
There are 10 sagas in total, each with its own opening quest that acts as a gatekeeper for the rest of the saga's content.

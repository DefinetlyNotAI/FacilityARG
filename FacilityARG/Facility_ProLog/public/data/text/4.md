**Whispers Beneath the Veil**  

Heaven, Hell-just painted signs,  
Tacked on doors with twitching spines.  
One reads *light*, the other *flame*,  
Both etched by hands too shy to name.

The choir hums, the pulpit sways,  
But silence feasts where echoes graze.  
Each whispered prayer, so soft, so dear,  
Returns malformed by **something near**.

A worshipper kneels, eyes sealed tight,  
Not in faith-but out of **fright**.  
For truth is sharp, and light reveals  
The wolves that purr with folded heels.

The serpent sings in sacred tone,  
Dripping grace from **broken bone**.  
He smiles with lips that stank of rust-  
And asks the flock to place their trust.

But trust is fuel, and faith is kindling,  
And sermons flare while shame is dwindling.  
The first to burn are those who knew  
The cost of lies painted as *true*.

So write these lines in soot, not stone-  
The faithful freeze the most alone.  
And know:  
**Heaven’s just a stage in white.  
Hell wears its halo out of spite.**
